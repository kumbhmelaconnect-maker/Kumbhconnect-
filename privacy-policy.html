What Information Do We Collect?

Automatic (Anonymous)

  • Anonymous Device ID (no name or identity)
  • App open time and date
  • Which pages/features were used
  • Search keywords (location names only)

Voluntarily Provided

  • Account Registration: Name, Email, mobile number (optional)
  • Grievance/Complaint: Name, mobile number, complaint details
  • If you create a Business/Volunteer/Guide Listing: business details, photos, contact info
  • If you send a Service Request: name, mobile, requested service and details
  • GPS Location — only after your explicit permission
  • Photos — for Media Center and Volunteer ID Card, optional (required for the Volunteer card)
🔑
Account & Login

Registration/Login is now required to use KumbhConnect (Email+Password or Google Sign-In).

  • Login uses Firebase Authentication (Google Cloud) — your password is never stored by us in plain form
  • You can permanently delete your account and all associated data (profile, listings) anytime from the "My Account" page
📍
GPS / Location Data

KumbhConnect never accesses GPS without your permission.

  • A clear consent popup is shown before any location access
  • Location is used only when submitting SOS or Grievance
  • Your live location is never shared automatically
  • Manual location entry is available if GPS is denied
💬
WhatsApp Sharing

Information is sent via WhatsApp only after your action — never automatically.

  • A consent popup is shown before sending a complaint
  • We always show: "Your information will be sent for nearby assistance"
  • SOS location goes via WhatsApp only after your consent
🚫
What We Never Do
  • ❌ We never sell your data or give it to advertisers
  • ❌ We never use data for advertisements
  • ❌ We never store your name or phone number in analytics
  • ❌ We never track cookies
  • ℹ️ Login, Media Center photo uploads, and Listing/Card creation use Firebase and Cloudinary (trusted technical service providers, not data brokers or ad networks) — see below
💾
Where Is Data Stored?
  • LocalStorage: On your own device — anonymous analytics events
  • Google Sheets (Apps Script): Anonymous event counts — no personal identity
  • Firebase (Google Cloud servers): Your account, login, Media Center photos, Business/Volunteer/Guide Listings, Service Requests, Reviews
  • Cloudinary (third-party image hosting): Stores photos uploaded via Media Center and the Volunteer ID Card
  • Grievances/SOS: Sent directly to Police/Guide via WhatsApp — we do not separately store these
📸
Media Center — Photo Uploads

Media Center lets you voluntarily upload photos to share with other pilgrims.

  • The name, city, caption, and location you provide are shown alongside your photo
  • Every photo requires Admin Approval before it appears in the public gallery
  • Photos are stored using Firebase (Google Cloud) and Cloudinary — both are trusted technical infrastructure providers, not advertisers or data brokers
  • Staff logins (Admin/Department/Press) use Email + Password, secured via Firebase Authentication
🏪
Business/Volunteer/Guide Listings & Reviews
  • A listing appears publicly only after Admin Approval
  • Reviews you leave after receiving a service are shown publicly (for trust and transparency)
  • Reviews can only be submitted for a genuinely completed service — this is technically enforced to prevent fake reviews
🔒
Offline & PWA Security

KumbhConnect is a Progressive Web App (PWA). In offline mode:

  • Emergency numbers and verified locations are safely cached
  • Local data is used when there is no network
  • Secure caching via Service Worker
