About KumbhConnect

Connecting Pilgrims, Sacred Places, and Digital Innovation

KumbhConnect is a digital platform that helps pilgrims discover information about Nashik Kumbh Mela, important places in Nashik and Trimbakeshwar, travel-related information, and available digital services. It also includes Kumbh AI, a conversational assistant that uses Artificial Intelligence to help users find information.

About the Project

What is KumbhConnect?

KumbhConnect is a Smart Pilgrimage Platform created for pilgrims visiting Nashik and Trimbakeshwar during the Sinhastha Kumbh Mela 2027. It brings maps, SOS assistance, guide services, and multilingual information together in one place.

Why it was developed

A large pilgrimage involves many places, schedules, and services. KumbhConnect was developed to make this information easier to find, so that pilgrims can plan their visit with greater confidence.

How it helps pilgrims

Pilgrims can explore places, find travel-related information and available services, and ask questions through a conversational assistant. The platform is designed for Marathi, Hindi, and English.

Nashik and Trimbakeshwar

Nashik, on the banks of the Godavari, and Trimbakeshwar, home to the Trimbakeshwar Jyotirlinga, are places of deep spiritual importance for the Kumbh Mela. KumbhConnect is built with respect for this heritage.

Artificial Intelligence at KumbhConnect

Kumbh AI is intended to help users access information through a conversational interface. Users can ask questions in everyday language.

Capability status

  • AvailableConversational assistance through a chat interface
  • AvailableInformation about places and locations
  • AvailableHelp finding hotels and service providers
  • AvailableMultilingual conversations
  • AvailableAnswers drawn from verified information supplied to the assistant

Responsible AI use

AI-generated responses may contain errors. Users should verify important travel, safety, and official information through appropriate authoritative sources.

Protect your privacy

Please avoid sharing personal information, such as identity documents, bank details, or contact numbers, with the AI assistant unless it is truly necessary.

Know its limits

Kumbh AI does not replace official authorities or emergency personnel. Please do not rely on it alone for emergencies, confirmed bookings, or real-time conditions.

Our Vision and Mission

Vision

To make pilgrimage-related information and digital guidance more accessible through a convenient, inclusive platform.

Mission

To help users discover relevant places and information through an accessible digital experience, while respecting the cultural and spiritual importance of Nashik Kumbh Mela.

Core Values

  • Devotion and Cultural Respect

    We approach the spiritual and cultural importance of Nashik Kumbh Mela with respect.

  • Accuracy and Responsible Information Sharing

    We aim to share clear information and to be honest about its limits.

  • Accessibility and Inclusion

    We aim to make the platform usable for people of different languages and abilities.

  • Privacy and Security

    We aim to handle user information with care.

  • Innovation and Responsible AI

    We use AI thoughtfully and encourage users to verify important information.

  • User Convenience

    We aim to keep the experience simple and easy to use on a phone.

Platform Objectives

  • Provide pilgrims with verified and up-to-date information
  • Provide SOS assistance features for emergency situations
  • Create opportunities for local guides, volunteers and businesses
  • Ensure smooth multilingual (Marathi, Hindi, English) usage
  • Make Nashik–Trimbakeshwar a Digital Information Hub
